01 — How it started

Every dress deserves another spin.

01

Selena ph began with a simple frustration: gorgeous dresses hanging unworn in closets while other women searched everywhere for exactly that piece. So we built the bridge. Our racks carry two kinds of dresses — brand-new pieces chosen from trusted suppliers, and pre-loved gems we inspect, launder and photograph honestly, one by one.

Nothing here is fast fashion filler. Whether it arrives with tags or with a history, every dress leaves us ready for a wedding, a date, a first day of work — whatever moment you're dressing for. And in sizes Small through Extra Large, there's a fit waiting for every body.

Then came the friendship. Selena ph met Sulit Dito MNL — a friendly online tiangge based in Holy Spirit, Quezon City — and we realized our customers were often the same women. They wanted dresses for occasions, and finds for everyday life. Why make them hop between two shops?

So we joined forces. One website, two boutiques, and a single promise: every peso you spend here is sulit. Every dress is curated with love, every find is tested with care, and every order ships from the same small team in Quezon City.

03 — What we stand for

The little promises we keep.

Whether you're buying a dress or a find, these are the rules that shape every order we ship.

03

We started small — just a Facebook page and a handful of regulars — and we grew because we kept our promises. Every dress is honestly described, even when "honestly" means pointing out a tiny flaw. Every gadget is tested, even when testing takes an extra hour. Every order is packed with care, even when the courier is already at the door.

The collaboration didn't change any of that. It just made it easier for you to find everything in one place. Same team, same standards, same sulit na sulit promise — now with twice the racks to browse.

Thank you for being part of our story. Whether it's your first order or your fiftieth, we're honored to dress you, equip you, and ship to your door.
