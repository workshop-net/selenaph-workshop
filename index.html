02 — About Selena ph

Every dress deserves another spin.

02

Selena ph began with a simple frustration: gorgeous dresses hanging unworn in closets while other women searched everywhere for exactly that piece. So we built the bridge. Our racks carry two kinds of dresses — brand-new pieces chosen from trusted suppliers, and pre-loved gems we inspect, launder and photograph honestly, one by one.

Nothing here is fast fashion filler. Whether it arrives with tags or with a history, every dress leaves us ready for a wedding, a date, a first day of work — whatever moment you're dressing for. And in sizes Small through Extra Large, there's a fit waiting for every body.
