01 — The Atelier

Every dress deserves another spin.

Selena ph began with a simple frustration: gorgeous dresses hanging unworn in closets while other women searched everywhere for exactly that piece. So we built the bridge.

01

Our racks carry two kinds of dresses — brand-new pieces chosen from trusted suppliers, and pre-loved gems we inspect, launder and photograph honestly, one by one.

Nothing here is fast fashion filler. Whether it arrives with tags or with a history, every dress leaves us ready for a wedding, a date, a first day of work — whatever moment you're dressing for. And in sizes Small through Extra Large, there's a fit waiting for every body.

We believe a dress should never hang unworn. Each one finds a new home, or a new beginning, with the woman who was meant to wear it next.
